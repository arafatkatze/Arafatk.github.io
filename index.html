A Suggested Journey

Follow this recommended itinerary as you travel across western India.

Day 1-3 — Delhi

Start in the Capital

Arrive in Delhi and immerse yourself in the old city. Walk through the narrow lanes of Chandni Chowk, taste legendary parathas at Paranthe Wali Gali, visit the solemn beauty of Humayun's Tomb, and watch sunset from the ramparts of the Red Fort.

Travel Day — Delhi to Udaipur

663 km · ~10 hrs by train / 1 hr flight

Board the Mewar Express or fly to Udaipur. The train journey passes through the arid beauty of Rajasthan, with glimpses of small desert towns and rolling sand dunes.

Day 4-6 — Udaipur

The Lake City

Explore the grandeur of the City Palace complex, take a boat ride on Lake Pichola as the Taj Lake Palace floats like a mirage, and wander through the colorful markets. Don't miss a sunset from the Monsoon Palace perched high above the city.

Travel Day — Udaipur to Mumbai

660 km · ~12 hrs by train / 1.5 hr flight

Travel south through Gujarat into Maharashtra. The landscape transitions from dry Rajasthani terrain to the lush Western Ghats before descending to the Arabian Sea coast.

Day 7-9 — Mumbai

City of Dreams

End your journey in India's maximum city. Stroll along Marine Drive at twilight, explore the colonial Gothic architecture of CST station, taste vada pav from street vendors, and take a ferry to the ancient Elephanta Caves carved into the island rock.
